Industrial Process Equipment Engineering in Baton Rouge

Nordstone provides engineered industrial process equipment solutions for customers in Baton Rouge and across the United States. Our engineering approach focuses on reliable equipment, practical design, safe operation, and long service life for demanding industrial applications.

Our capabilities support pressure vessels, storage tanks, heat exchangers, reactors, columns, process skids, boilers, HVAC systems, and BF valves. Equipment can be developed to suit project requirements, operating conditions, applicable codes, and installation needs.

For projects serving the Baton Rouge industrial market, Nordstone can support equipment engineering, manufacturing coordination, documentation, quality requirements, and supply activities from project planning through delivery.

The equipment range is intended for applications where durability, maintainability, and dependable performance are important. Detailed specifications can be developed according to the process, materials, pressure and temperature conditions, dimensions, and applicable standards.

Nordstone continues to support industrial customers throughout the USA with engineering-led process equipment solutions and responsive project coordination.
