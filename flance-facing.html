Flange Facing Services

In-Situ Flange Facing — Restore Joint Integrity Without Dismantling

We provide specialized In-Situ Flange Facing Services to restore damaged or out-of-tolerance flange faces directly at site — without dismantling pipelines or equipment.

Our service ensures perfect gasket seating, leak-free joints, and long-term flange integrity.

Why Flange Facing Is Critical

Flange joints are among the most failure-prone locations in piping systems. Even minor deviations cause serious problems.

  • Repeated gasket leakage
  • Loss of system pressure
  • Product loss and safety hazards
  • Environmental non-compliance
  • Extended unplanned shutdowns
Key Advantages

Why Choose Our In-Situ Flange Facing Services

No Dismantling Required

Work performed directly at site — no pipeline cutting needed.

Reduced Downtime

Significant reduction in shutdown duration compared to flange replacement.

Prevents Failures

Prevents repeat gasket failures by restoring original flange geometry.

Improved Reliability

Improves joint reliability through uniform bolt load distribution.

Versatile Service

Suitable for emergency response and planned maintenance.

Cost-Effective

Cost-effective alternative to full flange replacement.
