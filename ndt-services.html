Our NDT Methods

Six Core NDT Inspection Techniques

Primary Inspection Method

Visual Testing is the most fundamental NDT method using direct or remote visual examination to detect surface defects.

Detectable Defects
  • Surface cracks
  • Corrosion
  • Weld irregularities
  • Dimensional deviations
Standards

ASME Section V, API 570, ISO 17637

Volumetric Thickness Measurement

UT uses high-frequency sound waves to detect internal defects and measure material thickness.

Detectable Defects
  • Internal cracks
  • Voids
  • Inclusions
  • Laminations
  • Corrosion thinning
Standards

ASME Section V, API 5L, ASTM E114

Volumetric Weld Inspection

RT uses X-Ray or Gamma Ray radiation to produce images of internal weld quality.

Detectable Defects
  • Porosity
  • Slag inclusions
  • Incomplete penetration
  • Internal cavities
Standards

ASME Section V, API 1104, ASTM E94

Surface Defect Detection

PT uses a liquid dye to detect cracks and discontinuities open to the surface.

Detectable Defects
  • Cracks
  • Porosity
  • Laps
  • Cold shuts
  • Lack of fusion
Standards

ASME Section V, ASTM E165

Surface & Near-Surface Detection

MT detects surface and near-surface discontinuities in ferromagnetic materials.

Detectable Defects
  • Cracks
  • Seams
  • Laps
  • Fatigue damage
  • Grinding cracks
Standards

ASME Section V, ASTM E709, ASTM E1444

Advanced High-Resolution Inspection

PAUT uses electronically steered sound beams to generate real-time, high-resolution weld images.

Detectable Defects
  • Planar defects
  • Accurate sizing
  • Faster inspection
  • Digital records
Standards

ASME Section V Art.4 & 14, API 1104, ISO 13588
